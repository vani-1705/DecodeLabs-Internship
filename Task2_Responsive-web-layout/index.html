— Our Story —

Crafted with Love,
Rooted in Tradition

🍽️
🌿 🧑‍🍳 🏆

Since 2005, Thali House has been a celebration of India's rich culinary heritage. Every thali on our menu is a journey through a different region, crafted with age-old recipes passed down through generations.

We source our spices directly from farms across India and prepare everything fresh daily. No shortcuts, no compromises — just pure, authentic flavour on every plate.

  • 🐟🍛 Bengali Thali — A sweet-spicy journey through Bengal's soul, with fish curry and mishti doi that feel like home.
  • 🥘🫓 Gujarati Thali — Pure vegetarian bliss where every bite carries the warmth of jaggery and fresh farm spices.
  • 🍖🍲 Kashmiri Thali — A royal aromatic feast inspired by the saffron valleys — rich, slow-cooked and unforgettable.
  • 🫓🥣 Maharashtrian Thali — The coastal heart of Maharashtra on one plate — tangy, bold and full of character.
  • 🧈🫓 Punjabi Thali — Bold, buttery flavors from the land of five rivers — every bite is pure comfort food.
  • 🫓🥟 Rajasthani Thali — Desert royalty in every bite — dal baati churma made the old-fashioned way, nothing less.
  • 🍚🥥 South Indian Thali — A wholesome banana-leaf feast from the south — clean, fresh and deeply satisfying.
  • 🥞☕ Tiffins Thali — Light and healthy morning goodness — idli, upma, poha just like your grandmother made it.
